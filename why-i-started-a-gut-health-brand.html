Why I Started a Gut Health Brand When I Know Nothing About Gut Health

No degree. No lab coat. Just a guy who went down the rabbit hole and couldn't stop.

Let me just say that upfront.

I'm not a doctor. I'm not a nutritionist. I don't have a degree in anything health related. I have a background in customer service and a habit of asking too many questions.

That's it. That's the whole credential.

So why does a guy like that start a gut health brand?

Honestly? Because I got curious. And when I get curious about something I can't just read one article and move on. I have to follow it. Pull the thread. See where it goes.

And the gut health thread goes somewhere most people never expect.

It started with me. Nothing dramatic. Just the quiet realization that my stomach regularly doesn't feel great — and that I couldn't actually remember it always being that way. Something had changed. I just didn't know what. Or when. Or why.

So I started asking questions.

That was the mistake. The good kind.

Because once you start asking real questions about gut health — not the stuff on the back of a yogurt container, not the Instagram wellness posts, not the "eat more fiber" advice you've heard a hundred times — you find yourself in a completely different conversation. One that most people never have because nobody's having it out loud.

I started digging into actual research. Peer reviewed studies. Real science. And what I found genuinely surprised me. Not because it was shocking or scary. Because it was just sitting there. Available. Documented. And almost nobody was talking about it the way it deserved to be talked about.

The gut affects your energy. Your mood. Your skin. Your immune system. Your brain. The connection between what's happening in your digestive system and how you feel every single day is one of the most well-researched areas in modern health science — and most people have never heard any of it explained in plain language.

That bothered me.

I didn't set out to start a business. I set out to understand why I felt the way I felt. But somewhere in the middle of all that research I realized — if I'm finding this stuff and it's changing how I think about my own health, other people deserve to find it too.

So that's what Clean Gut Co. is.

Not a supplement brand. Not a diet plan. Not a wellness influencer telling you to cut out everything you love.

Just someone who got curious, did the research, and decided the information was too good to keep to himself.

You don't need credentials to care about people. You just need to actually care.

And I do.
