About Ryan

Not a doctor.
Just someone who got curious.

I'm not a doctor. I'm not a nutritionist. I don't have a degree in anything health related. I have a background in customer service and a habit of asking too many questions.

That's it. That's the whole credential.

So why does a guy like that start a gut health brand?

Honestly? Because I got curious. And when I get curious about something I can't just read one article and move on. I have to follow it. Pull the thread. See where it goes.

It started with my own stomach not feeling great, and then it kept going — my dad's health issues, my mom's type 2 diabetes, my own fatigue and fog. I started asking questions. That was the mistake. The good kind.

Because once you start asking real questions about gut health — not the stuff on the back of a yogurt container, not the Instagram wellness posts — you find yourself in a completely different conversation. One most people never have because nobody's having it out loud.

I started digging into actual peer-reviewed research. And what I found genuinely surprised me. The gut affects your energy, your mood, your skin, your immune system, your brain. That connection is one of the most well-researched areas in modern health science — and almost nobody explains it in plain language.

That bothered me.

I didn't set out to start a business. I set out to understand why I felt the way I felt. Somewhere in the middle of all that research I realized — if I'm finding this stuff and it's changing how I think about my own health, other people deserve to find it too.

So that's what Clean Gut Co. is. Not a supplement brand. Not a diet plan. Not a wellness influencer telling you to cut out everything you love.

Just someone who got curious, did the research, and decided the information was too good to keep to himself.

You don't need credentials to care about people. You just need to actually care. And I do.

— Ryan, Clean Gut Co.
